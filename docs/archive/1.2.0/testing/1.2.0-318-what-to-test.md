Thanks for helping test 1.2.0! This build focuses on dose logging, reminders, and making the app easier to use.

- Log a dose normally and with hold-to-log. Check that Undo removes the right entry and confirmations close after about four seconds.
- Pick a medication in History or Trends, then make sure quick log records that medication.
- Check your saved dose limits, reminders, and next-dose alerts.
- Try the time shortcuts, number pad, and rating sliders.
- Check larger text and Dark Mode, including toolbars and sheet close buttons.
- Try Demo Mode at the bottom of Settings > Data & Privacy. Names stay hidden until you confirm turning it off. It resets when the app relaunches.
- If you use backups or Lock Screen widgets, check those too.

If something looks wrong, gets stuck, or doesn’t save, please send feedback through TestFlight. Thanks!
