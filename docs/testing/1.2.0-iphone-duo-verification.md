# 1.2.0 iPhone Duo compatibility verification

October 28, 2026. Scope: the smallest necessary compatibility fixes and actual Duo screenshots. Existing layouts, four-tab navigation, model/storage formats, deployment target, capabilities and dependencies are retained.

## Findings and correction

The installed Xcode 27.2 (27B48) includes an actual iPhone Duo simulator, iOS 27.2 (24B91016s). Existing SwiftUI containers already resize between the 466×678-point cover, 951×669-point open inner display, and 669×951-point portrait inner display. At the 130-degree half-open pose the system relocates the presented form into the usable left region. No guessed hinge dimensions, device-name layout branches or new Duo-only APIs were needed.

Reopening the Duo rehosts presented sheets. In the baseline, an unsaved medication editor received a new model and lost its entered name/nickname; the presenting list model retained its identity. Temporary identity-only diagnostics established that boundary and were removed. The dose form also lost its reason. A disposable source-copy comparison verified the actual reason after scrolling back into view: it returned to its placeholder and Close no longer offered discard confirmation. Missing off-screen accessibility labels alone were not treated as proof of state loss.

The production correction is limited to input-state lifetime in six existing app files:

- Presenting list/detail views retain medication-editor sessions; list/detail/History retain dose-entry sessions. Recreated sheets receive the same in-memory draft for the same medication. Actual dismissal clears it; another medication receives a separate draft.
- The editor retains its discard baseline and original medication snapshot, preserving the existing quantity-edit baseline when stock changes while editing.
- Existing dose inputs and flags, including the in-progress logging guard, move together into the retained draft. Defaults, validation, calculations, event construction and persistence calls keep their existing semantics.
- A focused review found that the editor's view-local saving flag could reset during suspended persistence. The retained model now owns its saving guard and error presentation. Concurrent submission is rejected until completion, and a failed save permits a deliberate retry.

Two focused unit-test suites cover draft retention, reset, identity, original stock baseline, error state, and suspended-save duplicate prevention. No UI/snapshot test target, persistent draft storage, schema migration or new feature was introduced.

## Verification

- Baseline full regular-iPhone plan: **786 passed, zero failed/skipped**.
- Final focused editor/dose/atomic-save selection: **56 passed**.
- Final full plan: **792 passed, zero failed/skipped, 69 suites**, iPhone 17 simulator, iOS 26.5. Result bundle: `/tmp/asneeded-duo-final-verified.xcresult`; raw log: `/tmp/asneeded-duo-final-verified.log`.
- The suspended-save regression first failed with two persistence invocations while the first was pending. Its green result verifies one callback while pending and a retry after failure.
- Duo Add and existing Edit: entered text survives closed → half-open → open transitions. Discard confirmation survives; discarded nickname does not leak into the next editor.
- Duo dose form: the same reason survives all three poses after bringing the actual field into view. Discard confirmation remains. One synthetic dose was saved and appears once in History with its reason.
- History selection survives close/reopen and portrait rotation. Existing Medication, detail, History, Trends, Settings and Settings detail layouts were inspected in native screenshots.
- Largest accessibility text size in dark mode was inspected on Duo detail; the content remains scrollable. A regular iPhone 17 rendering check confirms its familiar compact layout and bottom tabs.
- Focused read-only review found no remaining important issues after the save-guard fix. `git diff --check` passes.
