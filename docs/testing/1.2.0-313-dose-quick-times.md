# 1.2.0 (313) saved-guidance color and dose-time refinements

October 28, 2026. Two explicitly requested refinements to the existing dose form.

- Clear / within-saved-guidance accents are semantic green in Log Dose and medication details. Caution and warning treatments remain unchanged. The green has separate light/dark values so badge text stays readable on the tinted card; the new contrast regression independently verifies a conservative tinted-surface bound at 4.5:1 or better. Dose-guidance assessment logic is unchanged.
- Quick dose times are Now, 30 min ago, 1 hour ago, 4 hours ago and 1 day ago. Each computes its timestamp at the moment of tapping. The one-day choice subtracts 24 elapsed hours. A grid wraps the five options according to available width and becomes one column at accessibility text sizes. Every preset has at least a 44-point control height. Existing dose draft/session ownership remains intact.
